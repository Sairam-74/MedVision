## Completion and interpretation

All 53 assigned retained panels were actually viewed at original detail: 19 E11 and 34 E12. Every row has its own observation, exact saved false-positive pixel count, all saved component boxes and the panel SHA-256 checked against the completed contact-sheet records. No row is a claim based solely on file generation or metadata. Together with the root's three separately recorded E11 normal views, the assigned tiny-normal follow-up coverage is 22 E11 and 34 E12; those three root views were not repeated here.

The full panels refine several coarse sheet-level placements, especially E11 3015 (ankle-region and outer forefoot points) and E12 0040 (shoulder-joint projection). The original sheet notes are preserved; these are explicit supplemental observations. Most tiny marks have visible locations, but a retained panel still does not establish their exact native boundaries. E12 0201 has no definite visible red region. E12 0260 and 2911 show at most faint color traces without a definite mask shape. For these three cases (0201, 0260, 2911), the saved evidence is respectively 15 pixels / box [1191,1552,1193,1563], 149 pixels / box [525,1416,546,1427], and 2 pixels / box [216,279,218,280]. These are nonempty saved predictions even when the retained JPEG cannot resolve their exact shape.

The root's E11 0970, 1263 and 3917 each have two saved native false-positive pixels with no definite red region resolvable on their panels. Its positive example E11 2586 has only one true-positive pixel, zero false-positive pixels and 465 false-negative pixels. That separate recorded example shows why removing tiny predictions cannot be assumed to improve reliability: tiny normal alerts coexist with tiny real target overlap. No size filter, threshold or clinical decision rule was changed or selected here.

Observed locations do not establish a clinical cause. Normal reference labels are unchanged; no radiologist adjudication, patient identities or fracture subtype labels were supplied. Maximum-pixel proxy scores are not calibrated fracture probabilities. No model tensor loading, new prediction generation, optimization, GPU work, calibration or locked-test access occurred. Saved panel JPEGs were decoded for these actual visual reviews, and panel files were byte-hashed; the original dataset archive/images and mask arrays were not decoded for this follow-up.
